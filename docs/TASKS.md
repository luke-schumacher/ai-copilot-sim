# Group 1 tasks

Hours are for the whole group. Level: Bachelor = for Bachelor students, Master = led by a Master student, Shared = Bachelors and Masters together.
Every student: 10 hours onboarding, 75 hours on the tasks below, 35 hours meetings, report and talk: 120 hours.
Start in week 1, without hardware: tasks 1, 3 (documentation first), 4 (method on laptops), 5 (mock stream), 6 (pipeline on mock data), 9 (dashboard on replayed data) and 10 (models on a laptop).
Group 2's tasks are in the `ai-copilot-lab` repository.

| # | task | what you do | in → out | done when | hours | level | depends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Onboarding** | Get to know the plan for the rig, the rules and the repository. Run the mock data stream on your own laptop and read the setup guide once from start to finish. | In: repository, setup guide. Out: everyone has run the mock stream. | Everyone has run the mock stream and passes a short quiz on the data rule. | 60 (6×10) | Shared | none |
| 2 | **Rig bring-up and setup guide** | Set up the delivered rig: the PC, wheel and pedals, the three simulators and the network. Correct and extend the setup guide as you go so that someone new can repeat it. Make a backup image of the PC. | In: delivered rig, setup guide. Out: rig in a known-good state, corrected guide, backup image. | Someone who has not touched the rig sets up all three simulators from the guide alone. | 30 | Bachelor | rig delivery (mid-November at the earliest) |
| 3 | **Telemetry readers for the three simulators** | Write a small program for each simulator that reads speed, throttle, brake, steering and position while you drive, at a fixed rate. Test it on saved sample data first, then on the real simulator. Measure how often each simulator really updates. | In: documentation of the three simulators, sample data. Out: three readers and a table of fields, units and update rates. | Each simulator records a 10-lap session without gaps, and the real update rates are written down. | 60 | Shared | none: start with the documentation |
| 4 | **Clocks and timestamps** | Every recorded value needs a time stamp you can trust. Measure how far the clocks of the simulator PC, the evaluation PC and the AI machine disagree and drift apart, and decide which time each value is stamped with. | In: the three machines on the switch. Out: timestamp policy, measurement script, results. | Offset and drift of all three machines measured over one hour, with the error stated. | 40 | Master | none: try the method on laptops first |
| 5 | **Data flow with ROS 2** | Send the telemetry from the simulator PC to the other machines with ROS 2, a toolkit for passing messages between programs. Define the message, record the stream and replay it with its original timing. | In: mock stream, later the real readers. Out: message definition, bridge, recording and replay. | A recorded stream replays with its original timing and time stamps. The message is agreed with Group 2 by 13 November. | 40 | Shared | none: mock stream first |
| 6 | **Data pipeline** | Build the path the data takes after capture: store every session in one agreed layout, check its quality (gaps, jumps, impossible values), cut it into laps and make it easy to replay. The dashboard, the lap files and the AI work all build on it. | In: recordings from task 5. Out: stored, checked sessions and a quality report. | One command takes a recording to a checked, stored session, and a report lists every problem found. | 40 | Shared | tasks 3 and 5 (start on mock data) |
| 7 | **Lap files and labelled sessions** | Convert stored sessions into the lap-file format that Group 2's checker reads and check them with Group 2's validator. Then drive at least 10 test sessions following Group 2's driving cards, for example 'brake 10 m earlier at turn 3', and label each one. | In: stored sessions, Group 2's driving cards and validator. Out: lap files and 10 labelled sessions. | 10 labelled sessions of 6 or more laps pass the validator and load in the checker. | 35 | Bachelor | task 6; Group 2's tasks 2 and 7 |
| 8 | **Different setups and the delay model** | Run the chain in at least three setups, for example everything on one PC, simulator PC to evaluation PC, simulator PC to AI machine, and measure delay, jitter and lost data in each. Then build a SimPy model, a Python tool for simulating events over time, of those delays and check it against your measurements. | In: tasks 4 and 5. Out: measurements per setup, a calibrated delay model, value ranges for Group 2. | The model reproduces the measured median and 95th-percentile delay in two setups. The ranges are handed to Group 2. | 65 | Master | tasks 4 and 5 (start with assumed values) |
| 9 | **Dashboard on the evaluation PC** | Build a dashboard for the second monitor that shows a live or replayed session: speed, throttle, brake and lap times, the delay of the data chain, and the output of the AI model. | In: stored sessions from task 6, delay figures, model output. Out: dashboard. | The dashboard shows a live session and a replay, updates at least five times a second and runs on the evaluation PC. | 35 | Shared | task 6 (start with replayed mock data) |
| 10 | **Local AI: choosing and testing models** | Set up the AI machine and choose a local language model for it. Compare at least three models on the same tasks, for example turning a sentence into a structured test and summarising a lap. Measure speed, memory use and answer quality, and recommend one. | In: AI machine, candidate models. Out: model comparison and a running model server. | A written comparison of three models with measured speed and quality, and a recommendation. The chosen model answers requests from the simulator PC. | 55 | Master | AI machine delivery (start on a laptop) |
| 11 | **Cameras, raw inputs and heart rate** | A later stage. Record two cameras with time stamps, read raw wheel and pedal data, and read the Polar H10 chest strap (heart rate, and an ECG trace, the heart's electrical signal). Align everything with the telemetry. | In: tasks 4 and 5, cameras, wheel base, pedals, chest strap. Out: aligned recordings of video, inputs and heart rate. | Each source is recorded and aligned with the telemetry, and the alignment error is stated. | 50 | Shared | tasks 4 and 5 |
| 12 | **Meetings, report and final talk** | Weekly team meetings, the weekly pull request, a short report and the final presentation. | In: all results. Out: report of at most 12 pages and a talk. | The report is reviewed by Luke and the talk has been rehearsed once. | 210 (6×35) | Shared | all |
